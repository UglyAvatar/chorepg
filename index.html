📜 Chore RPG: 30-Minute Game Loop Guide

Turn real-life household productivity into an epic tabletop campaign! Work together to complete tasks, stock up your dice inventory, and face monsters in the encounter phase.

Step 1 • Set Up Roster

Manage Party & Heroes

Select your Party Size (1 to 4 players)—encounter DC targets scale automatically! Tap ✏️ on any slot to change hero names, classes, or icons. Save heroes to your library or tap 🗑️ Delete to remove extra profiles.

Step 2 • The Work Phase

Complete Household Quests

Spend 20–25 minutes clearing real-world chores. Checking off quests rewards your party with bonus dice (d4, d6, or d8) for your total roll pool.

Step 3 • The Encounter Arena

Face the Encounter

Switch to the Encounter Arena tab during rest time. Select your enemy Threat Level, tag an MVP hero, and unleash your collected dice pool against targets scaled to your party!

Step 4 • Backup & Restore

Export & Import Campaign

Use the 📥 Export and 📤 Import buttons in the header bar to back up your campaign file or move your saved progress between devices.

Step 5 • AI Story Companion

Generate Custom Campaign Hooks

Click ✨ Generate AI Hook in the Encounter Arena to pull custom fantasy scenarios using Google Gemini. Get a free key instantly at Google AI Studio, paste it once when prompted, and let the AI write your adventure context!
